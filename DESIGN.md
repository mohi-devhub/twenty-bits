# Replay visual direction

Make the comparison read like an annotated natural-history spread: a warm paper canvas, deep green ink, strong table rules, and a large serif title above two precisely aligned fields of candidates. The questions and the shrinking hypothesis space are the focal content. There is no decorative illustration.

Instrument Serif gives the title a quiet editorial voice. Manrope carries names, controls, and measurements. Both fonts are bundled under their OFL licenses. Green identifies the random policy, and burnt orange identifies the greedy policy. Color is paired with text labels and outcome states.

Each panel holds its policy name, a remaining-candidate or effective-candidate count, a 4-column grid, the current exact question, its observed answer, and the current EIG. The target can be revealed. Under noise, opacity represents posterior mass; zero mass alone means elimination. A timeline moves both players by action index and holds a completed player's state.

One deterministic replay clock drives the question text, candidate opacity, answer, and metric readouts together. Reduced-motion users can step or scrub directly. The export holds every action long enough to read and includes an ending pause.
