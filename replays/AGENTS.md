# Replay artifact rules

This folder contains the offline replay viewer and its HyperFrames composition. Follow the repository's phase gates and budget rules. Do not publish or contact Tinker.

Use the pinned HyperFrames CLI in package.json. The timeline is deterministic and registered under window.__timelines.replay. Questions, answers, and posteriors come from games.json. Keep two or three players matched to the same record and target, as requested for the final study exports. Noiseless zeros can be eliminated; noisy shading represents positive posterior weights. The study folder mirrors the shared viewer code and assets through scripts/build_study_replays.py. It does not run a model. The user explicitly requested local GIF and MP4 exports; render them after local checks without another approval gate.

Run the composition check and inspect desktop, narrow-screen, and exported frames after visual changes. Exports are requested by the user's Phase 1 brief and may be regenerated locally. Keep example selection disclosed in README.md. Preserve the bundled font licenses.
